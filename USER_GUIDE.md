## Step 1: Create an Account
Before tracking, you need to create a secure personal profile.

1. **Fill in Details:** Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, and a secure **Password**.
2. **Password Requirements:** For your security, your password must contain:
   * At least **8 characters**
   * At least **one capital letter** (e.g., `A`)
   * At least **one number** (e.g., `5`)
   * At least **one special character** (e.g., `@`, `!`, `#`)
3. **Verify Your Email:** 
   * A 6-digit code will be sent to the email address you registered.
   * Tap on the visual code boxes on your screen to bring up the keyboard, type the 6 digits, and tap **Verify**.
   * *If the code does not arrive, wait 30 seconds and tap **Resend Code**.*

## Step 2: Connect Your GPS Tracking Device
To track your assets, you must link your hardware tracking device to your account.

1. Locate your device's **IMEI number** (a unique 15-digit number printed on the device label, its packaging, or its barcode).
2. You can link the device in two ways:
   * **Option A: Scan QR Code:** Tap **Scan QR code**, allow camera permissions, and point your phone's camera at the QR code sticker on your GPS device.
   * **Option B: Manual Entry:** Type the 15-digit IMEI number directly into the input field and press **Pair device**.

---

## Step 3: Test the Device Signal
Once paired, the app will test the connection to ensure the device is transmitting GPS coordinates correctly.

* The app will show a **"Waiting for device signal"** pulsing indicator for up to 30 seconds.
* **Connection Successful:** You will see a green checkmark (`✅`go) stating **"Device connected!"**. The app will automatically move to the vehicle registration screen.

### 🔍 Connection Troubleshooting
If the app shows **"No signal detected"**, check the following:
1. **Power:** Ensure your GPS device is turned on and properly powered.
2. **SIM Card:** Verify that a valid SIM card is correctly inserted into the device with an active data/credit plan.
3. **Sky Visibility:** If possible, take the device outdoors or place it near a window so it can connect to the cellular network and GPS satellites.
4. Tap **Try again** once ready.

---

## Step 4: Register Your Vehicle
You must link your newly paired GPS device to a specific vehicle.

Fill out the form fields:
1. **Vehicle Nickname:** A friendly name of your choice (e.g., *Office Pickup*, *Mom's Sedan*).
2. **Number Plate:** Your vehicle's official registration plate (e.g., *RAC 123 A*).
3. **Make:** The manufacturer (e.g., *Toyota*, *Hyundai*).
4. **Model:** The model of the vehicle (e.g., *Hilux*, *Elantra*).
5. Tap **Save vehicle** to complete the registration.

---

## Step 5: Select a Subscription Plan
To start monitoring your vehicle, you need an active tracking service plan.

### 💳 Plan Tiers
* **Free Trial:** 14 Days (Limited to 1 vehicle).
* **Basic Plan:** 30 Days (Supports up to 3 vehicles) — **5,000 RWF**.
* **Fleet Plan:** 30 Days (Supports unlimited vehicles) — **15,000 RWF**.

### 📱 How to Pay (via Mobile Money or Card)
1. Select the desired plan and tap **Subscribe** (or **Upgrade/Renew** from the home screen).
2. A secure payment window will pop up.
3. Input your Mobile Money number (MTN MoMo, Airtel, etc.) or card details.
4. Approve the payment prompt on your phone.
5. Your subscription will instantly activate, completing your setup!

## Step 6: Dashboard & Tracking Overview
Once your setup is finished, you will land on the **Dashboard** home screen.

### 🚗 Vehicle Status Indicators
On your dashboard, you will see a summary card displaying status statistics:
* **Moving:** Vehicles currently driving (speeding above 5 km/h).
* **Idle:** Vehicles with their engine running but currently stationary.
* **Parked:** Vehicles turned off or offline.
* **No Response:** Devices that haven't sent a signal update in the last 15 minutes.

### 🗺️ Real-Time Tracking
* Tap the **Tracking** tab to see your vehicle's live position plotted on a map.
* Tap the **Vehicles** list to view specific information, battery charge levels, and historical data about your cars.
* Tap the **Alerts** tab to view notifications, speed warnings, or geofence crossings.
* A status banner at the top of your dashboard displays your plan name and remaining days. If it expires, simply tap **Renew Now** to top up immediately.
